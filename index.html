What is NAMI?

NAMI is the national alliance on mental illness. NAMI is a grassroots mental health organization dedicated to building better lives for the millions of Americans affected by mental illness.

Our alliance includes more than 650 NAMI state organizations and affiliates who work in your community. NAMI advocates for access to services, treatment, supports, and research and is steadfast in its commitment to raising awareness and building a community of hope for all of those in need.

What is Nami Emory?

NAMI Emory is a non-profit organization and local afffiliate of NAMI that provides education, support, and advocacy for individuals and families affected by mental illness in the Emory community and beyond.
