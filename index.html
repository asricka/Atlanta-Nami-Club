Who we are

NAMI is the national alliance on mental illness. We are a grassroots mental health organization dedicated to building better lives for the millions of Americans affected by mental illness.

Our alliance includes more than 650 NAMI state organizations and affiliates who work in your commpunity. NAMI advocates for access to services, treatment, supports, and research and is steadfast in its commitment to raising awareness and building a community of hope for all of those in need.
