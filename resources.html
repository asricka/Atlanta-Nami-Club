  • Counseling and Psychological Services CAPS

    Students who are experiencing a mental health crisis call the CAPS after Hours Line at 404-727-7450 and press 1. CAPS expanded after-hours support provides Emory students with access to confidential assistance 24 hours a day, 7 days a week (24/7), year-round. Now, a student can call CAPS and speak immediately with a mental health professional to identify their needs, get support, and help develop a plan for next steps.
